Why a ledger, not just a model

A score by itself doesn't recover anything. Reverra closes the loop: predict, act, confirm, and feed the real outcome back in.

Built as one pipeline
Score, draft, and retrain share the same data — not three disconnected tools wired together after the fact.
See features →
Honest about its data
No public dataset of real recovery outcomes exists, so Reverra's data is simulated, calibrated to published industry recovery rates and deliberately noisy, rather than taken from real customer records.

Three steps, one loop

From a failed charge to a confirmed outcome that makes the next prediction better.

01

Score

Each failed payment gets a recovery probability from a model trained on the ledger.

02

Act

Reverra drafts the recovery email that fits the failure reason, so effort goes where it pays back.

03

Confirm

Log the real outcome; it joins the training data and the model retrains.

Features

What's in the ledger

Four entries, each doing one job.

Score the failure
Plan, amount, tenure, and failure reason go in; a recovery probability comes out, trained on failure-reason patterns in the data.
scikit-learn
Draft the recovery email
Gemini writes a message suited to the customer and the failure, with urgency set by the model's own score — not a fixed template.
Gemini API
Ask Rivera
A floating assistant answers questions like "which failure reason loses us the most" by looking up real numbers, not guessing. It also helps you find your way around, and handles finance and general-knowledge questions.
FastAPI
Confirm and retrain
Entered payments sit as pending until someone confirms what actually happened — only confirmed outcomes ever reach the training data.
live retrain
Docs

How a payment moves through it

One direction, four stops — from raw failure to a tracked outcome.

01
Failure lands
A payment fails. Plan, amount, tenure, and reason are recorded.
02
Model scores it
The classifier returns a recovery probability from the current data.
03
Ledger acts
A drafted email goes out; the entry sits as pending on the dashboard.
04
Outcome confirmed
Recovered or not — the real result joins the dataset for the next retrain.

No public dataset of real failed-payment outcomes exists — that data lives inside processors like Stripe and isn't released. Reverra's data is simulated: its recovery rate is calibrated to published industry ranges (roughly 20-40%), and the failure-reason mix and effects are assumptions, not measurements. The pipeline, the time-based evaluation, and the retraining loop around it are real, and the same columns accept real data.
